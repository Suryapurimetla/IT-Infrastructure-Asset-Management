# 10. Risks and Mitigation

## 10.1 Testing Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Delayed implementation | Reduces available testing time. | Prioritize critical features and test alongside development. |
| Unclear requirements | May result in incomplete test cases. | Review the SRS and clarify requirements before testing. |
| Insufficient test data | Important scenarios may remain untested. | Prepare valid, invalid, and boundary-value sample data. |
| Environment issues | May prevent application execution. | Verify dependencies, configuration, and database setup. |
| Undetected defects | May affect system reliability. | Perform functional, integration, and regression testing. |
| Security weaknesses | May expose data or permit unauthorized access. | Test access restrictions, input validation, and data protection where implemented. |
| Limited testing time | May leave some cases unexecuted. | Prioritize critical requirements and document untested cases. |

## 10.2 Risk Monitoring

The team will review risks throughout testing and update mitigation actions when necessary. Unresolved high-impact issues will be documented in the final test report.

## 10.3 Residual Risks

Incomplete features, unavailable environments, and unexecuted test cases will be documented as limitations. They will not be reported as successfully tested.
