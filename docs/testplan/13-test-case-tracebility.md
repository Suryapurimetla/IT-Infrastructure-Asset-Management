## 13. Test Case Management & Traceability

RTM ensures mapping of SRS requirements to test cases.

Example:

- ITAM-F-001 (Asset registration) → TC-ITAM-01, TC-ITAM-02
- ITAM-F-002 (Asset search) → TC-ITAM-03
- ITAM-F-003 (Asset update) → TC-ITAM-04
- ITAM-F-004 (Asset allocation) → TC-ITAM-05
- ITAM-F-005 (Warranty and license tracking) → TC-ITAM-06, TC-ITAM-07
