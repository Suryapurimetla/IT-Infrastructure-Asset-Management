# 2. Test Items

The following modules and features of the IT Infrastructure & Asset Management System are planned for testing, subject to their inclusion in the approved requirements and availability in the implementation.

## 2.1 Asset Inventory Management

Testing will verify the registration, viewing, searching, updating and removal or deactivation of asset records.

## 2.2 Hardware and Software Management

Testing will verify that hardware and software information can be recorded, viewed and updated correctly.

## 2.3 Asset Allocation Management

Testing will verify asset assignment, allocation status and availability tracking, where these functions are implemented.

## 2.4 Software License Management

Testing will verify the recording and retrieval of software license details and the tracking of license expiry dates.

## 2.5 Warranty Management

Testing will verify the recording of warranty information, warranty dates and expiry status.

## 2.6 Maintenance Management

Testing will verify the creation, updating and retrieval of asset maintenance records.

## 2.7 Alerts and Notifications

Testing will verify configured alerts for license expiry, warranty expiry and scheduled maintenance, where implemented.

## 2.8 User Interface and Access Control

Testing will verify input validation, user interactions and access permissions for the available user roles, where applicable.

## 2.9 Error Handling

Testing will verify that invalid inputs and expected application errors are handled appropriately without corrupting asset records.
